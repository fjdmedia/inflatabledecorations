Backdrops & Balloon Garlands

Balloon Backdrops & Garlands in Winnipeg

Every eye in the room lands on one spot first — the backdrop is built to be that spot, with balloons clustered in real clumps of different sizes instead of spaced out like a store-bought strip.

Start your inquiry

One focal point, not a wall of balloons

A backdrop and garland set gives a room a single centre — the spot every phone points at the moment someone walks in. Guests gather there, the cake table goes there, and most of the photos come from there.

The garland itself is clustered by hand in mixed sizes rather than spaced evenly along a strip, which is the difference between a room that looks designed and one that looks decorated.

Pick the panel, or skip it

The backdrop is a wooden panel, and it comes in more than one silhouette: a classic arch, a wavy edge, a treat wall, or a metal hoop frame. If the wall is already the feature, the garland runs straight across it and skips the panel entirely.
