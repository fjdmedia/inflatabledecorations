Baby & Bridal Showers

Baby & Bridal Shower Balloons in Winnipeg

A shower is usually the first time everyone sees the room. A backdrop and garland give it a focal point, a place for the photos, and colours pulled straight from your theme.

Start your inquiry

Built Around Your Colours

Send the invitation, the theme, or just the two or three colours you have in mind, and the decor gets built around them. Soft and neutral, bright and playful, or something closer to a colour story you already picked — it is made to order either way.

Custom vinyl lettering can be added to a backdrop panel: a name, a date, or the line from the invitation.

The Corner Everyone Photographs

Almost every shower ends up with one spot where the photos happen. A backdrop panel with an organic garland around it is built for exactly that — it gives guests somewhere to stand, and it frames the gift table, the cake, or the guest of honour.

Themed foil balloons and florals can be worked in where they suit the theme.

Baby Showers

Baby showers are the most common setup here. Sage and cream, blush and gold, or a full colour theme — the backdrop and garland get built to match, with room for a custom name or a “oh baby” panel if you want one.

Bridal Showers

For bridal showers the same setup runs a little more formal: an arched backdrop, a garland in your palette, and lettering for the bride’s name or the date. It works as the photo wall for the afternoon and then comes down the same day.

Common Questions

What does shower balloon decor usually cost?

A full backdrop-and-garland setup delivered and installed starts at $350. If you would rather hang it yourself, a Grab & Go garland starts at $90. Final pricing depends on the size of the setup and the space it is going in, so send your details for a real quote.

Do you set it up and take it down?

Yes. Full-service setups are installed before your guests arrive and taken down afterwards. Grab & Go garlands are the exception — those are assembled and ready for you to pick up and hang yourself.

Can you match a specific theme or colour?

Yes. Send the invitation, a photo, or the colours you have picked, and the decor is made to order around them. Custom vinyl lettering and themed foil accents can be added.
