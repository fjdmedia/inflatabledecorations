Birthday Balloon Decor in Winnipeg

First birthdays, themed parties, and the milestone ones with a number in front of them. The decor gets built to whatever the theme is, and it is up before anyone walks in.

First Birthdays

First birthdays get photographed more than almost any other party, so they are usually built around one strong focal point — a backdrop panel with a garland, sized for the cake table or the corner where the photos will happen.

Custom lettering can carry the name and the theme, whether that is a golf cart and a “hole in ONE” sign or something else entirely.

Themed Parties

The theme drives the build. A strawberry first birthday, a space theme, a candy land party, a diner setup with burgers and a checkered floor — the colours, the foil shapes and the custom panels all get made to match whatever your kid picked.

Send the theme and any inspiration photos you have. The design comes back built around them rather than pulled off a shelf.

Milestone Birthdays

For the birthdays with a number attached, a balloon number stack gives you the number itself as the centrepiece, in your colours, sized to be the photo.

Number stacks pair well with a garland or a backdrop panel if you want the whole corner done.

Set Up Before Anyone Arrives

Setups are installed ahead of the party and taken down afterwards, so the day itself is not spent inflating balloons or climbing on a chair with tape.

Common Questions

What does birthday balloon decor usually cost?

A backdrop-and-garland setup delivered and installed starts at $350. A balloon number stack on its own starts at $70. What it lands at depends on the size of the setup and the space, so send your details for a real quote.

Can you build a specific theme?

Yes. The theme drives the design — colours, foil shapes and custom panels are all made to order. Send the theme and any inspiration photos and the design gets built around them.

Do you set up and take down?

Yes. Setups go in before the party starts and come down afterwards, so you are not dealing with it on the day or cleaning it up that night.
