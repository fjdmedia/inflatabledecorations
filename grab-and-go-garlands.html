Grab & Go Balloon Garlands in Winnipeg

A smaller amount of balloon decor, assembled in your colours and ready to collect. You handle the transport and the hanging, which is what keeps it the lighter option.

Start your inquiry

A smaller, more flexible option

Grab & Go garlands suit birthdays, showers and celebrations that want a balloon focal point without a full installation — a smaller space, a simpler event, or a decor budget you would rather keep modest.

They are still professionally assembled. What changes is who carries it and who hangs it, and both of those are yours.

What to expect

Each garland is made to order in your chosen colours and can be built to suit a theme. Themed foil balloons and other specialty accents can be added for an additional cost.

It will be finished and waiting at your scheduled pickup time. Bring a vehicle big enough to carry it safely — depending on the size of the garland that can mean a larger vehicle, or a cleared-out back seat.

If you would rather not handle it

Delivery and professional installation come with the full-service garland setups instead.
